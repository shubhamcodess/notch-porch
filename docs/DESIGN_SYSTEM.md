# Design system

Notch Porch is meant to feel like a part of macOS. These are the rules every new piece should follow. The tokens live at the top of [`src/renderer/styles.css`](../src/renderer/styles.css).

## Principles

1. **Quiet.** It sits in dead space and should never shout. Small, soft, dark.
2. **Native.** System font, system-feeling motion, Apple-style controls.
3. **Merged with the hardware.** Every surface fades to pure black at the top so a card looks like it grows out of the notch.
4. **Honest data.** Real audio drives the visuals; real YouTube data fills the lists. No placeholders dressed up as content.

## Colour

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--accent` | `#ff375f` | Equalizer (outside Album colors), active shuffle and like, the lyrics button |
| `--text` | `rgba(255,255,255,.96)` | Titles, primary labels |
| `--text-2` | `rgba(255,255,255,.5)` | Secondary labels, artist line |
| `--text-3` | `rgba(255,255,255,.32)` | Hints, time stamps |
| Card (dark) | graphite gradient, `#000` at the top | Default theme |
| Fills | `rgba(255,255,255,.055)` resting, `.11` hover | Chips, rows, fields |

### Themes

- **Dark.** A black-to-graphite gradient, opaque enough to read on any wallpaper.
- **Liquid glass.** A blurred, translucent version of dark.
- **Album colors.** A two-colour gradient taken from the cover (darkened, and desaturated for warm hues so reds and oranges don't glare), with two large soft blobs drifting slowly behind it. The equalizer takes a soft tone of the cover.

## Type

System font only: `-apple-system` (SF Pro). No web fonts, no emoji in the interface.

| Role | Size / weight |
|---|---|
| Card title | 15 px / 600, tight tracking |
| Artist or lyric line | 12 px / 400 (lyric 500, brighter) |
| Chips and rows | 12.5 px / 500 and 13 px / 600 |
| Caption under the notch | 11.5 px / 400 |
| Time stamps | 10 px, tabular numbers |

## Space and size

| Thing | Size |
|---|---|
| Card | 380 px wide, 176 px tall (268 px in search) |
| Corner radius | 26 px for the card, 10 px for fields and chips |
| Side items | 22 px at rest, 30 px with the card open |
| Distance from the notch | 10 px at rest, 22 px with the card open |
| Controls | 38 px, with a 44 px play button |

## Motion

One easing for almost everything: `cubic-bezier(.32, .72, 0, 1)` (`--ease`), a fast start with a long, soft landing.

| Motion | Duration |
|---|---|
| Card open / close | 0.42 s |
| Background fade-in | 0.38 s |
| Side items easing apart on hover | 0.5 s |
| Rolling behind the notch | 0.7 s, with a full turn |
| Lyric line change | 0.34 s out, 0.5 s in, 10 px of travel (opacity and transform only) |
| Colour changes in Album colors | 1 s |

Avoid blur filters in animations; this window is transparent and they stutter.

## Pages and the switcher

- **Cards flip.** Pages turn about their vertical axis (62° with a little scale). A two-finger horizontal swipe drives the flip live, so the card follows your fingers; past the halfway point it completes, otherwise it springs back. Tapping an icon flips too.
- **Page icons, not dots.** Each page has a 12 px stroke glyph (manifest `icon`) under the content, evenly spaced from the content above and the card edge below. The active one is bright and slightly larger, and a small accent pip marks a page that needs attention.
- **Height.** The card is 184 px tall when there is more than one page (176 px with one), so the icon row has room.

## Components

- **Control button.** Round, translucent; the play button is white with a dark glyph. *On* state is a coloured glyph with no background.
- **Chip.** A 28 px rounded rectangle in the same shape as the search field, very faint fill, no icon, no dot.
- **Row.** 34 px rounded art, a 13 px bold title, an 11 px grey subtitle; the whole row is the target.
- **Section label.** 11 px, 600, uppercase-free, 42 % white.
- **Caption.** Text on a soft dark fade that is transparent at the top (no seam against the notch) and peaks just beneath the line. Always centred.
- **Pulse line.** A 1 px hairline under the notch in the cover's vivid colour, breathing slowly and flickering with the bass.
- **Loader.** A small ring inside the play button.

## Iconography

Inline SVG, stroke icons at 16 px with 2.2 px strokes and round caps, or solid glyphs for transport. The menu-bar icon is a template image so macOS tints it for light and dark bars.
